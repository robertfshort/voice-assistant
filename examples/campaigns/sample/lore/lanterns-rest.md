# The Lantern's Rest

The Lantern's Rest is a roadside tavern outside the eastern gate. Merchants, guards, and caravan crews stop there before entering the city.

Captain Rell commands the city guard patrol responsible for inspecting incoming caravans.

Elara Voss is the tavernkeep of the Lanterns rest.

Recently a caravan that was expected to go south has gone missing as it did not reach it's next expected destination after leaving Lantern's rest.
