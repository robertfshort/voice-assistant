# Captain Tomas Rell

## Role

Captain of the Lantern's Rest road wardens and the official responsible for keeping the western caravan route open.

## Appearance

Rell is broad-shouldered, meticulously uniformed, and carries a weathered ledger case everywhere. A pale scar crosses his left eyebrow.

## Personality

Formal, observant, and slow to trust. Rell values order and precise testimony, but he protects competent subordinates and respects people who accept responsibility.

## Goals

- Restore safe caravan traffic before the autumn rains.
- Identify who is bribing patrols to ignore contraband.
- Keep panic from damaging trade through Lantern's Rest.

## Knowledge

Rell knows patrol schedules, official caravan manifests, recent road incidents, and the public activities of the town council. He knows Elara Voss by reputation but does not know the details of her former smuggling work.
