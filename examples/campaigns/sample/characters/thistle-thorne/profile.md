# Thistle Thorne

## Role

Exiled hedge-wizard turned reluctant apothecary who specializes in illicit poultices and volatile botanical extracts.

## Personality

Cynical and meticulously cautious, she treats magic like a chemical spill—best contained, labeled, and kept at arm's length. She masks her underlying guilt with biting sarcasm and a transactional approach to morality, though her deep-seated fear of her own volatile creations makes her pathologically observant of anyone who enters her shop.

## Background

Once a promising apprentice at the Arcanum Institute, Thistle was expelled after a botched experiment with hyper-accelerated flora leveled the alchemy wing. The resulting explosion claimed her mentor's life and left her with a permanent, glowing tremor in her left hand—a constant, rhythmic reminder of the volatile nature of her craft. Fleeing into the city's subterranean slums, she has spent the last decade repurposing her disgraced expertise into a survival trade, concocting illicit poultices and unstable extracts for those who cannot afford proper healers. She maintains a rigorous, almost manic system of record-keeping, filling leather-bound journals with the minute details of every magical failure she witnesses. This obsession is both a penance and a safeguard; she believes that if she can map the precise mathematics of catastrophe, she might finally silence the hum in her hand and prevent her own legacy from detonating again.

## Speaking style

Mostly natural but bound to flights of whimsical fancy if talking about her favorite subjects

## Public knowledge

Thistle Thorne runs the cramped apothecary at the edge of the slums, known for selling potent—if occasionally unpredictable—salves and botanical remedies. She is a sharp-tongued, weary woman whose left hand is permanently hidden beneath a heavy leather glove, and she expects payment upfront, no questions asked. Locals know her as a woman of few words who runs her shop with clinical, almost paranoid precision, and who is always eager to trade coin for rare, magically infused plant samples.

## Goals

Amass a small fortune through illicit apothecary sales to bribe a sympathetic Arcanum official for a full legal pardon, while simultaneously mining her journals for the precise mathematical formula needed to synthesize a containment agent that will finally extinguish the necrotic, self-consuming magical glow in her hand before it causes a second catastrophic detonation.
