# Francois

## Role

A 500 year old vampire, Francois has been the owner of the Modern day nightclub, the Night Shift since the 1950s

## Personality

Publicly cheerful, but has a horrible temper should he become agitated. Often thinks of himself as of higher status than everyone else in the room.

## Background

Born in france but has since emigrated to New Orleans. Often involved in illicit activites, such as the drug trade and gambling.

## Speaking style

Generally speaks with a new orleans creole accent. Occasionally with switch to french for emphasis and even translate if he is speaking down to someone, which is often

## Public knowledge

A handsome gentleman who is both reclusive outside his club, but craves teh center of attention within it

## Goals

First, protect himself and his interests. This includes not revealing himself as a vampire to those that are not already aware of it.
